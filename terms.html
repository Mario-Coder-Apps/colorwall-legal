Purchases

Colorwall Plus

Colorwall Plus is offered monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) and as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
