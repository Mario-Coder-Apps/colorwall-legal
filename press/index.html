In one paragraph

Colorwall is a paint-by-numbers app for adults who want a quiet few minutes. It has no advertising at all - no banners, no videos between pictures - no account and no tracking. The pictures are realistic, detailed illustrations rather than cartoon pages, and every one is a vector page that stays crisp however far you zoom in. Four season books are free, along with a new picture every day and a bigger one every week.

Description

Many coloring apps pay for themselves with an ad after nearly every picture. Colorwall takes the other route: it is calm on purpose. Pick a picture, tap a number and fill it in area by area, with soft sounds and haptics and no timer. Or switch the numbers off and choose your own colors.

Pictures come in three levels - Easy for a short break, Medium for an evening, Hard for a long one. Finished pictures replay as a timelapse you can share, and hang in frames on your own gallery wall.

Four painting styles change how a picture looks while you fill it: Flat and Colored pencil are included, Watercolor and Painted come with Colorwall Plus.

How the pictures are made

Each motif starts as an illustration from an image model. The developer's own pipeline turns it into a numbered vector page with its own palette and sorts it into Easy, Medium or Hard by the number of areas.
